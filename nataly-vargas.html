The person behind the practice

A personal beginning.
A purpose to share.

As shared in her REC spotlight, Nataly first tried Pilates because of her own posture and low back pain. She found that the practice helped her feel better physically and mentally—and wanted to keep exploring it.

Her path into the studio began at the front desk. That experience eventually inspired her to complete teacher training and become an instructor herself.

Today, she brings that personal connection to teaching, with a belief that healthy movement should be accessible to everyone.
