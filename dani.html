With a background in athletics and law enforcement, Dani knows how to bring focus and effort to a workout. As shared in her REC spotlight, her goal is to push you to work hard while helping you feel supported.

Her teaching spans Yoga Sculpt, CrossFit, and Lagree. Her Instagram bio identifies her as a Level 1 Lagree instructor at Venom Lagree and a Yoga Sculpt instructor at Yoga by Degrees.
