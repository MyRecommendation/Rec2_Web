The person behind the coaching

Service. Education. Strength.

Sports first sparked Chris’s interest in training. He later served in the U.S. Marine Corps before pursuing degrees in exercise science and nutrition and wellness.

Today, he leads Delta Sport Fitness & Nutrition in Aurora. His background connects physical training with nutrition education, helping people work toward their individual goals.
