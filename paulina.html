The person behind the workout

Encouragement is her thing.

Originally from Chile, Paulina has spent 20 years in fitness, with experience spanning barre, yoga, strength, and functional fitness. Today, you can find her coaching at SHRED415 Naperville.

She’s a Chicago Marathon finisher, a mom of four, an elementary multilingual teacher, and a fashion and wellness creator for women 50+. Whether she’s in the classroom or the studio, encouraging people to believe in themselves is at the heart of what she does.

What to expect from her classes

Expect a workout with personality: great energy, great music, and the occasional burst of singing or dancing while she encourages you through the work.
