Fitness on the Green at Block 59

Summer Sundays, outdoor movement, and a chance to meet the people who make a class feel special. Block 59’s Fitness on the Green series brought local studios and the community together in Naperville.

REC joined a DRIPYOGA session with a table on site, meeting attendees and introducing them to a place to discover and recognize their favorite instructors. DRIPYOGA was the featured studio; REC participated alongside the class.
