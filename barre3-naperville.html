Meet Kelli Fuhr.

Studio owner Kelli Fuhr discovered barre3 through an online class and wanted to bring the experience to her community. Her background includes teaching children’s yoga, personal training, and nutrition coaching. With her husband Dennis’s support, she brought that enthusiasm for movement and balance to the studio.

The teacher makes the experience.

A helpful modification, a clear cue, or a little encouragement can change how a class feels. Look for your instructor’s name when you book, and give yourself time to introduce yourself before your first session.
