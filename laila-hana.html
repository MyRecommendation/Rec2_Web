Her approach

Challenging.
Always approachable.

Laila’s classes bring attention to how you move. With a focus on anatomy, alignment, and control, she encourages students to build strength while feeling more connected to their bodies.

“Learn to connect with your core. All movement starts from your core.”

— Laila’s teaching philosophy, shared in her original REC spotlight

Known professionally as Laila Ruangpan, she also teaches aerial silks and sling. Her Aloft Circus Arts coach bio describes a background in piano and dance, alongside aerial coaching at Aloft and Frankfort Circus Center.
