The person behind the practice

More than the poses.

Mel has been teaching yoga since early 2023. Whatever the class style, she wants students to feel welcome, move with intention, and connect with their breath.

Her positive energy, approachable teaching style, and post-class chats reflect what she loves about yoga: building community, having fun, and meeting people exactly where they are.
