Carry that strength with you.

Sue has been teaching since 2012. In her original REC story, she shared how yoga has brought friendships, connections, and a community of people into her life.

Her hope is that students leave class knowing the strength they discover on the mat can support them through the rest of their day. Her CorePower teacher bio echoes that connection between practice and everyday life.

Her original REC feature introduces her as Sue Lee; you’ll find her listed as Sue L on CorePower’s teacher page.
