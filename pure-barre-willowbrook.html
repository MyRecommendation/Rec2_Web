Meet Jamie.

Jamie began teaching Pure Barre in Evanston in 2013. After teaching in Naperville and Wheaton, she became Willowbrook’s general manager in 2017 and took ownership in 2023. Her background includes a BFA in Dance from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas.
