Meet Field House.

Just outside Madison, Field House brings mat Pilates, yoga, and strength classes together in a bright, welcoming studio.

Middleton · Madison area

Movement with room for community.

Field House offers music-led classes for people looking for mindful movement and connection. Its approach welcomes different experience levels, with Pilates, yoga, and strength options under one roof.

The team is led by co-founders and instructors Bo and Emily. Get to know the people behind the studio, then choose a class that fits your practice.
