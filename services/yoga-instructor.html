Questions before your first booking.

Can I explore instructors without downloading REC?

Yes. Read profiles, view photos and recommendations, and follow studio booking links on this website. Download the REC App when you want to recommend someone in the app.

Which Naperville yoga class should a beginner choose?

Ask the studio which current class welcomes first-time students and what instruction it provides. Class labels vary. Our first yoga class guide includes questions to ask before booking.

Can I book a yoga class on this website?

REC links you to the studio’s website or booking options. Reservations, payments, cancellations, and schedule changes are handled by the studio.
