The person behind the practice

Yoga to carry into your everyday life.

Claudine began practicing yoga in 1999 and has been teaching since 2004. Her approach makes room for connection, conversation, and the lessons that stay with you long after you roll up your mat.

At MBS, her classes are described as music-driven, with an emphasis on breath, alignment, and mindful movement. Clear instruction and a grounding presence help students build confidence in their practice.

She also teaches Sunday mornings at SHRED415 Naperville. Her Shred to Recovery class brings together the studio’s treadmill and floor format with intentionally sequenced stretching and mobility.

Her approach

  • Breath
  • Alignment
  • Mindful movement
  • Music

Alongside weekly classes, Claudine offers workshops and private instruction. She also hosts The Tattered Mat podcast, extending the conversation beyond the studio.
