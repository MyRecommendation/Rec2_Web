The person behind the training

A runner’s perspective.
A strength coach’s approach.

Heather began her fitness career at SHRED415. With a background in running, she became interested in the role resistance training could play in supporting the activities she loved.

She later expanded her focus to one-on-one training and nutrition coaching. Today, she co-owns The SET in Western Springs and offers online coaching through BodyLabxHeather.

Her own coaching site highlights the importance of mindset, personalized programming, and accountability alongside training.

Areas of focus

  • Strength training
  • Functional movement
  • Mobility
  • Nutrition coaching
  • Pre/postnatal training

Her official SET bio lists ACE personal training certification, functional and mobility training, nutrition coaching, pre/postnatal specialization, and Lagree training.
