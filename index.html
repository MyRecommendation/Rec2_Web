A few things to know

Start here. Keep discovering.

Do I need the app to explore these instructors?

You can read the instructor spotlights, watch their videos, and follow studio schedule links right here on the website. Download REC to discover and recommend people in the app.

Where is REC growing?

We’re building our fitness community in Naperville and across DuPage County, including Downers Grove, Wheaton, Glen Ellyn, Lisle, and Villa Park. You’ll also find spotlights from beyond the area.

Can I book a class through REC?

Our instructor pages link to studio schedules and instructor booking websites. You book directly with the studio or instructor and should confirm their current class times.

Is REC available on my phone?

REC is available for iPhone and iPad on the Apple App Store. It is free to download, with in-app purchases available.
