Meet Balance.

Owner Beth Adas and the Balance team bring yoga, Pilates, and community gatherings together under one roof. The studio’s name reflects the idea of finding effort and ease in your practice. Non-heated yoga and small equipment classes offer different ways to build a routine that feels like yours.

Find your way into class.

Yoga

Gentle, Hatha, Flow, Prenatal, and Restorative classes offer different ways to practice.

Equipment Pilates

Small groups of up to six use the reformer, tower, and chair, with attention from the teacher.
