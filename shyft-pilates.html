A smaller studio. Personal attention.

Shyft is a boutique Pilates studio on Jefferson Avenue in downtown Naperville. Its approach centers on small classes, personalized coaching, and a welcoming community.

Meet Marlissa Hamgeri.

Shyft’s owner and master Pilates instructor grew with the studio, first teaching and then managing it before her family took over in 2025.

Planning your first visit.

The studio recommends its Support or Mixed-Level options for new clients, depending on their experience and preferred pace. Ask Shyft which current class suits you.

Grip socks are required. Bring comfortable fitted clothing and water; the studio provides equipment. Booking requires a Shyft MINDBODY account and waiver.
