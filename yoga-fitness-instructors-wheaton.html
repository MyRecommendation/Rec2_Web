Questions before your next class.

Are all of these instructors yoga teachers?

This guide covers different formats: Mel teaches yoga, Dani’s featured Wheaton option is Yoga Sculpt, and Kristen teaches Lagree. Use each profile and studio description to choose the method you want.

Can a beginner try these studios?

Ask the studio which current session is appropriate for your experience. Confirm the level, pace, equipment introduction, and instructor before booking. Inclusion in this guide does not mean every class is designed for first-time students.

Can I book through REC?

REC helps you discover the instructor and points you toward official booking options. Reservations, payments, and cancellations are handled directly by the studio.
