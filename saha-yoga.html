Meet Saha.

Saha was founded around the shared experience of practicing yoga. Its classes connect breath and movement, with different levels of intensity and heat. The studio describes a space where students can practice, learn, and grow together.

Find your way into class.

26 Hot+2 & Hott Mixx

Heated practices, including a set sequence of 26 postures and two breathing exercises, and a blend of Hatha, Vinyasa, and Yin.

Saha Flow

A moderate-intensity practice with flowing sequences, breath work, and meditation.

Yin & Ashtanga

Long-held passive poses in Yin, or a progressive sequence connecting breath and movement in Ashtanga.
