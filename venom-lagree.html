Get to know the Lagree method.

Venom teaches Lagree: a strength-based workout using slow, controlled movement on a spring-loaded Lagree machine. Its sessions are 45 minutes, with an instructor guiding the movements and transitions.

Lagree is a distinct method from Pilates. Read Venom’s explanation of the workout and ask the studio which first class suits your experience.

Your first visit.

Venom asks first-time students to arrive 10–15 minutes early, bring water, and wear comfortable, close-fitting clothing. Grip socks are required. Arrive with time to meet your instructor and get familiar with the machine.
